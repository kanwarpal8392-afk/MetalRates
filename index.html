Frequently asked questions
Why do gold rates differ between cities?

Local taxes, transport costs, demand and jeweller margins create small differences from one city to another.

What is the difference between 24K and 22K gold?

24K is about 99.9% pure gold. 22K is about 91.6% gold mixed with other metals for strength, so it is used for jewellery.

Is GST charged on gold?

GST of 3% applies on the gold value, and making charges attract GST as well.

How do price alerts work?

Set a target and keep this page open. When the rate crosses it, you see a message and, if you allowed notifications, a browser alert. Alerts are stored only in your browser.

How our rates are calculated

We publish daily Indian retail reference rates for gold, silver and platinum, and show the date they were last updated at the top of the page. International spot prices and the USD to INR rate are shown for reference when available. City-wise differences are approximate. If the rates are more than a day old, the page says so clearly. Platinum has no widely published Indian benchmark, so its rate is an estimate from the international price.

Indian retail rates also include import duty, GST, making charges and local jeweller margins, so the price you pay can differ from the rate shown. Always confirm with your jeweller or dealer. See our Disclaimer.
